## Site text (erdosproblems.com/617, fetched 2026-08-26)
Let r≥3. If the edges of $K_{r^2+1}$ are r-coloured then there exist r+1 vertices with at
least one colour missing on the induced $K_{r+1}$ ("no balanced colouring"). Erdős–Gyárfás:
proved for **r=3 and r=4**, observed **false for r=2**, and fails for infinitely many r when
$r^2+1$ is replaced by $r^2$. OPEN for general r.

## Lean reading
Main theorem: ∀ coloring `Sym2 V → Fin r` of V with |V| = r²+1, ∃ S of size r+1 and a color k
with no pair of S colored k. Faithful to the site sentence-for-sentence (loop colors s(v,v)
are quantified over in the hypothesis type but excluded by `u ≠ v` in the conclusion — inert).
Variants r_eq_3 / r_eq_4 match the two published cases; variants.r2 matches the r² remark.

## Computation (three engines; all anchors agree)
Define BAL(n,r) = ∃ r-coloring of K_n where every (r+1)-set sees all r colors (= negation of
the conjecture's conclusion). Engines: (A) Python DFS w/ snapshot-undo + forward checking;
(B) C DFS (star order, first-occurrence symmetry breaking); (C) z3 SMT-LIA with running-max
symmetry breaking; plus a boolean DIMACS encoding (z3 -dimacs).

DB-SANITY GATE (all passed):
- BAL(4,2) & BAL(5,2) = **SAT**: explicit witnesses found by A and B (identical node counts
  8 resp. 37), witnesses re-validated independently set-by-set. This reproduces the site's
  "false for r=2" exactly.
- BAL(6,2) = **UNSAT** by A, B and both z3 encodings (R(3,3)=6 ✓).

Main results:
- **BAL(9,3) = SAT** (A: 13.3M nodes/78 s; B agrees): explicit balanced 3-coloring of K₉,
  validated 126/126 four-sets see all three colors. Consistent with the EG r²-side failure.
- **BAL(10,3) = UNSAT** (z3/SMT with symmetry breaking, 3 m 35 s) — computational
  confirmation of ErGy99's r=3 theorem on K₁₀. The pure DFS exhaustion exceeded budget
