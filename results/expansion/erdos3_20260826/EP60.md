## Site text (erdosproblems.com/60)
"Does every graph on n vertices with > ex(n;C₄) edges contain ≫ n^{1/2} many copies of C₄?"
Open; He–Ma–Yang prove it for n = q²+q+1 (q even); Erdős–Simonovits conjectured ≥ 2 copies.

## Lean reading
Main: ∃c>0 ∀ᶠn ∀G, extremalNumber n C₄ < |E(G)| → c·√n ≤ #{H' ≤ G : H'.coe ≃g C₄}.
Asymptotic (∀ᶜ over unbounded n) ⇒ not bounded-evaluable; classified, not attacked.
Copy-counting set {H' : G.Subgraph | Nonempty (H'.coe ≃g cycleGraph 4)} counts each C₄
exactly once via its 4-vertex subgraph ✓ matches standard copy counts.
Variant `two_copies` (∀ᶠn, ≥2 copies above ex) is the E–S conjecture, filed `research solved`
in-file — presumably citing a proof; we did not verify that proof, only tested small n.

## Computation (exact, two independent implementations agreeing after a caught bug)
#C4-as-subgraph = ½·Σ_{u<v} C(|N(u)∩N(v)|,2); enumeration of all graphs per n.
Impl-1 bitmask popcount; impl-2 networkx common-neighbors. Impl-2 initially used a
per-4-subset edge-count formula (m=4→1) which miscounts triangle+pendant subsets as C₄s —
caught by comparing thresholds; replaced by the pairwise formula; full agreement after.

| n | ex(n,C₄) | min #C₄ among graphs with > ex edges | at \|E\| |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 4 | 1 (diamond) | 5 |
| 5 | 6 | 2 | 7 |
| 6 | 7 | **1** | 8 |
| 7 | 9 | **1** | 10 |

- Single-C4 witnesses validated by an orthogonal cycle enumerator (nx.simple_cycles,
  deduplicated by edge-set): e.g. n=6 witness = star(0→{1..5}) ∪ {14,15,23}.
- DB-sanity: OEIS A006856 was checked and is the *girth ≥ 5* sequence (a(3)=2 excludes
  triangles), NOT ex(n,C₄) — flagged to prevent future cross-contamination in campaign
  lookups. Our ex values are consistent with the classical exact table (ex(6,C₄)=7 via
  C₅+twin-attachment; verified independently).
- Data note: exactly-one-copy graphs sit immediately above ex at n = 4, 6, 7 — the
  two-copies heuristic has no small-n shadow; its content is genuinely asymptotic.

## Verdict
Faithful & open (main), correctly separated variants; no defect. Exact brackets to n=7.
