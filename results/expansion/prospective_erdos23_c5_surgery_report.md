# Erdős 23 prospective `C5` blow-up and surgery trial

## Outcome

`HOLD_BOUNDED`.

The exact current formal statement was inspected before the trial was frozen.
After the database gate passed, 574 canonical nonuniform independent-set
`C5` blow-ups and three distinct eligible triangle-free surgeries were solved
exactly. There were no crossings and no timeouts.

This is negative evidence around a major human conjecture, not a disproof or a
public/release candidate.

## Current formal reading

`FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/23.lean` tags `Erdos23.erdos_23` as
`research open`. It asks whether every triangle-free graph on `5n` vertices
can be made bipartite by deleting at most `n^2` edges. For a graph `G`, the
minimum required number is

```text
beta_edge(G) = |E(G)| - maxcut(G).
```

The formal source separately marks the 25-vertex case solved and records the
balanced independent-set `C5` blow-up as tight. The starting carrier
`complement(C5[K4])` is precisely the balanced blow-up `(4,4,4,4,4)`:
`|E|=80`, `maxcut=64`, and `beta_edge=16=20^2/25`.

## Exact database gate

The gate evaluated 33 triangle-free controls, all padded to an order divisible
by five when necessary. It included all connected triangle-free five-vertex
Atlas graphs, balanced blow-ups through order 25, Petersen, and complete
bipartite controls. No graph crossed. The balanced blow-ups reproduced the
expected equality cases.

## Nonuniform blow-ups

The frozen search evaluated every canonical dihedral class generated by part
sizes 1 through 8 at orders 15, 20, and 25, for 574 graphs total.

- crossings: zero;
- timeouts: zero;
- equality cases: exactly `(3,3,3,3,3)`, `(4,4,4,4,4)`, and
  `(5,5,5,5,5)`;
- no nonbalanced graph had slack one or two.

The computed values admit an independent exact check. If the five independent
parts are `A_i`, then

```text
beta_edge(G) = min_i |A_i| |A_(i+1)|.
```

Indeed, a maximum cut has an optimum assigning every false-twin part wholly to
one side, because the objective is linear in that part's assignment once the
others are fixed. Every two-coloring of the odd base cycle leaves a
monochromatic interface. Conversely, deleting a smallest interface turns the
base cycle into a path and hence makes the blow-up bipartite. This independent
formula matched all 574 computational records.

## Triangle-free edge surgery

Only the three balanced walls met the frozen near-wall threshold. All their
nonedges have a common neighbor, so adding any nonedge creates a triangle and
is rejected before invariant evaluation. Up to isomorphism, deleting one edge
gives one graph at each order. Their bipartization numbers are respectively
`8`, `15`, and `24`, exactly one below the bounds `9`, `16`, and `25`.

These values also have a solver-independent proof. Deleting the remainder of
the affected interface gives the upper bound `m^2-1`. Any smaller
bipartization of `B-e`, together with the restored edge `e`, would yield a
bipartization of the tight balanced graph `B` using fewer than `m^2` edge
deletions, a contradiction.

## Interpretation

Within the frozen family, balance is the unique extremal geometry. Moving any
mass nonuniformly creates at least three edges of safety at the tested orders,
and every permitted one-edge triangle-free mutation moves inward rather than
outward. The carrier is therefore an exact equality calibration for Erdős 23,
but this local geometry supplies no counterexample direction.

The adversarial candidate and novelty gates were not triggered because no
strict crossing appeared. No commit, push, release, issue, PR, or public action
was taken.
