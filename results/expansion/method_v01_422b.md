## Phase 0 — frozen target and provenance

The current WoW II page marks 422b `O` and prints, dated December 8, 2010:

> Let G be a connected graph on n > 3 vertices and M the vertices of maximum
> degree. Then i(G) <= alpha(G[M]) + gamma(G[V-M])^2.

Primary page:
`http://cms.uhd.edu/faculty/delavinae/research/wowII/open.html`.
The target occurs in the repository snapshots
`data/wowii-conjectures.json` and `corpora/graffiti_pc_wow2.json`.

The page's own definitions freeze the single reading:

- `i(G)` is the minimum cardinality of an independent dominating set;
- `M` is the set of all maximum-degree vertices;
- `alpha(H)` is the independence number of the indicated induced subgraph;
- `gamma(H)` is the domination number of the indicated induced subgraph;
- `gamma(empty)=0`, the standard totalization needed for regular graphs.

The stray extra right parenthesis printed after `G[M]` is typographical and
does not create a second mathematical reading. Outcome class: `UNAMBIGUOUS`.

The contemporaneous paper E. DeLaViña, R. Pepper, and B. Waller,
*Graffiti.pc on the Independent-Domination Number of a Graph* (Congressus
Numerantium, 2011; dated 2010 in the PDF) was recovered from
`https://www.uhd.edu/documents/academics/sciences/dpw-cn2011.pdf`. Its glossary
confirms the same meanings of `i`, `alpha`, induced-subgraph notation, and
maximum-degree vertices. It discusses 422a and 422c but does not state, prove,
or refute 422b.

At upstream `google-deepmind/formal-conjectures` main commit

## Residual and theorem baseline

Use the signed residual

```text
R(G) = alpha(G[M]) + gamma(G[V-M])^2 - i(G).
```

The conjecture asserts `R(G) >= 0`.

For every regular graph, `M=V`, so

```text
R(G) = alpha(G) - i(G) >= 0.
```

This is the elementary theorem baseline that every independent dominating set
is independent and hence has cardinality at most `alpha(G)`. Equality occurs
when every maximal independent set has maximum size. It explains the repeated
regular-family wall and proves that no regular carrier or uniform blow-up of a
regular quotient can cross 422b.

## Quotient reduction and obstruction

Let `Q[K_s]` be a positive clique blow-up with blob sizes `s_v`. Then exactly:

```text
i(Q[K_s])                         = i(Q)
alpha(Q[K_s][M])                  = alpha(Q[M])
gamma(Q[K_s][V-M])                = gamma(Q[V-M])
deg(blob v) + 1                   = s_v + sum_{u~v} s_u.
```

Thus the weights affect the residual only by selecting the maximum weighted
closed-degree blob set `M`. Uniform weights on a regular quotient force `M=V`
and remain behind the theorem baseline.

### Pre-search directional prediction

To decrease `R`, nonuniform weights must make a proper set `M` uniquely
maximum while:

- `alpha(Q[M])` stays small, preferably because `Q[M]` is a clique;
- `gamma(Q[V-M])` stays one, so the complement of `M` has a universal vertex;
- `i(Q)` remains at least three.

At quotient order at most nine, the exact search also measures the maximum
observed `i(Q)`. Whenever `i(Q) <= 4`, a negative residual necessarily has
`gamma(Q[V-M])=1`, since nonempty `M` gives `alpha(Q[M])>=1` and
`gamma>=2` would already make the right side at least five.

The max-set realizability constraints are

```text
z_v = s_v + sum_{u~v} s_u
z_m = t                 for m in M
z_x <= t - 1            for x outside M
s_v >= 1, sum_v s_v <= 100.
```

A numerical integer optimizer is permitted only to propose weights. Every
positive witness is rechecked with exact integer arithmetic. Solver
infeasibility is not used as an exact negative certificate.
