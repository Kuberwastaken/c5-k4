```text

(n,m,girth,lambda_max,tree,target,R141) = (15,45,4,6,8,7,1),
target = girth // 2 - 1 + lambda_max.
```

The three-sheet permutation experiment collapsed to the cyclic parent lane:
every cotree variable lies alone on a short fundamental cycle, so fixed-point-
free monodromy forced all variables into `A3 ~= Z3`.

This addendum changes only the sheet number and permutation group.  In `S4`
there are nine fixed-point-free elements: six 4-cycles and three double
transpositions.  They do not form a subgroup, and pairs among them can fail to
commute.  The frozen trial therefore permits genuinely nonabelian short-cycle
monodromy while preserving the same base graph and invariant target.

## Exact deterministic CSP

Use the seed's deterministic integer labelling and lexicographic BFS spanning
tree.  Gauge-fix every tree edge to the identity.  For each of the 31 sorted
cotree edges `u<v`, assign one of the 24 lexicographically ordered image tuples
in `S4`.  The lift contains

```text
(u,s) -- (v,p_uv(s));
```

reverse traversal uses `p_uv^-1`.

Enumerate the same 423 simple base cycles of length four or five.  For every
oriented cycle, require its ordered permutation product to be fixed-point-free.
Thus its monodromy may be a 4-cycle or a double transposition, and the lifted
cycle cannot close in four or five steps on any sheet.

To prevent another abelian collapse, also require the completed cotree
assignment to contain two permutations that do not commute, and require the
subgroup generated by all assigned cotree permutations to act transitively on
the four sheets (equivalently, the lift is connected).  These are frozen
applicability conditions, not post-result filters.

The solver is exact finite-domain CSP:

1. domains are 24-bit masks and group operations use complete exact `S4`
   multiplication/inverse tables;
2. cycle constraints are propagated in `(arity, original cycle index)` order;
3. generalized arc consistency is computed by exhaustive local assignments,
   with memoization by constraint and domain tuple;
4. reject a partial node if no pair of remaining domain values can witness
   noncommutativity;
5. select the first nonsingleton cotree variable in sorted-edge order and
   branch on permutation ranks in increasing order;
6. accept only a leaf satisfying all cycle products, noncommutativity, and
   transitivity requirements.  The first leaf is therefore lexicographically
   minimal among applicable lifts.

The exact search cap is 250,000 branch states and 55 wall-clock seconds.
Complete exhaustion is `INFEASIBLE`; either cap is `SOLVER_TIMEOUT`.  No
randomization, alternate seed, fifth sheet, weakened monodromy constraint, or
retuning is allowed after evaluation.
