%N Home primes: for n >= 2, a(n) = the prime that is finally reached when you start with n, concatenate its prime factors (A037276) and repeat until a prime is reached (a(n) = -1 if no prime is ever reached).

%C The initial 1 could have been omitted.

%C Probabilistic arguments give exactly zero for the chance that the sequence of integers starting at n contains no prime, the expected number of primes being given by a divergent sequence. - _J. H. Conway_

%C After over 100 iterations, a(49) is still composite - see A056938 for the latest information.

%C More terms:

%C a(50) to a(60) are 3517, 317, 2213, 53, 2333, 773, 37463, 1129, 229, 59, 35149;

%C a(61) to a(65) are 61, 31237, 337, 1272505013723, 1381321118321175157763339900357651;

%C a(66) to a(76) are 2311, 67, 3739, 33191, 257, 71, 1119179, 73, 379, 571, 333271.
