A113252
Corresponds to m = 6 in a family of 4th-order linear recurrence sequences given by a(n) = m^4*a(n-4) + (2*m)^2*a(n-3) - 4*a(n-1), a(0) = -1, a(1) = 4, a(2) = -13 + 6*(m-1) + 3*(m-1)^2, a(3) = (-8+m^2)^2.
