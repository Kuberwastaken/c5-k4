Ico (T(n+1)−1) (T(n+2)−1) − a(n+1)` where T = triangular numbers: step n uses
exactly n new primes; cumulative T(n). Hand-checked: a(2)=2·3−1=5,
a(3)=5·7·11−5=380, a(4)=13·17·19·23−380=96197, a(5)=29·…·43−96197=58546472 —
all four Lean test theorems reproduce. The recurrence is the unique sensible
reading of "smallest integer such that a(n)+a(n−1) has the first n distinct
prime factors not used before" (the sum must be divisible by that product and
the product is its minimum, forcing equality).
