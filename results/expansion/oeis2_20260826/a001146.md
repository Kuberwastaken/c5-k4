# A001146 — audit (2^(2^n); Hasler's k⁴−1 | 2^k−1 characterization)

**Class: HOLD_BOUNDED.** Gates: a ✓, b ✓, c ✓ (exact exhaustive scan), d ✓.

OEIS A001146: a(n) = 2^{2^n}; Hasler's comment (Jul 25 2015) conjectures that
{n : k = a(n), n>1} are exactly the k with k⁴−1 | 2^k−1 (intersection of
A247219 and A247165). The Lean file proves the forward direction
(`divisibility_fact`, research solved, no sorry) and formalizes the reverse
direction as research open — faithful to the source split.

## Verification

Exhaustive exact scan of even k ≤ 2·10^7 (odd k impossible: k⁴−1 divisible by
8 while 2^k−1 is odd — proved filter): the only solutions of k⁴−1 | 2^k−1 are
k ∈ {16, 256, 65536} = {2^{2^n} : n ≥ 2} within range. No counterexample;
any counterexample exceeds 2·10^7. Script: `a001146_primary.py` (plus the
inline rerun fixing that script's dead-loop bug).
