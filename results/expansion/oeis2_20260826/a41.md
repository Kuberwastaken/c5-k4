# A000041 (OEIS/41.lean) — audit (Sun: no perfect-power partition numbers)

**Class: HOLD (audited, no defect).** Gates: a ✓, b ✓, d ✓.

## Source vs declaration

OEIS A000041 (`oeis_pages/A000041.html`, recovered 2026-08-26):

> %C Conjecture: No a(n) has the form x^m with m > 1 and x > 1. - _Zhi-Wei Sun_, Dec 02 2013

Lean @ `2411d22e`: `noPowerPartitionNumber : answer(sorry) ↔ ∀ k, ¬IsPerfectPower (a k)`
with `a n = Fintype.card (Nat.Partition n)` — exactly the OEIS claim. The
quantifier-scope difference (Sun states it for n > 1; Lean for all k ≥ 0) is
immaterial because Mathlib's `Nat.IsPerfectPower` requires both factors > 1, so
a(0) = a(1) = 1 is excluded automatically. Test values 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 match.

## Status check

Still genuinely open and actively researched: Merca–Ono–Tsai,
*Do perfect powers repel partition numbers?*, arXiv:2501.03754 (2025) confirm
Sun's conjecture "in many cases" via Bennett–Siksek prime-power gaps; Ono,
arXiv:2510.19164 (2025) proves repulsion for truncated products p_B; a 2026
RIMS paper studies the growth of near-misses. No proof or disproof of the full
conjecture anywhere.

## Conclusion

Faithful declaration of an open conjecture. No repair, no crossing.
