## What the Erdős database tracks

The canonical Erdős Problems data separates three fields that must not be
collapsed:

- `informal_status`: the accepted mathematical standing of the prose problem;
- `formal_status: Lean`: a solution has been formally checked;
- `formalized: yes`: a statement has been translated, whether or not it has a
  proof.

DeepMind adds a fourth coordinate at declaration granularity: the exact Lean
statement's category, proof link, answer scope, and sibling variants. These
coordinates can lag or differ. A merged proof can cover one variant of a prose
problem without resolving its headline, and the canonical database can lag a
reviewed DeepMind merge. Novelty therefore requires exact-scope comparison and
an all-state audit, not one status label.

## Finite graph candidates and the missed #617 lane

Among the source-classified finite graph candidates, 23, 64, 128, 628, and 742
already had local coverage or a proved-domain stop. Erdős 617 had been omitted
because its complete-graph edge colouring is encoded as `Sym2 V -> Fin r`, not
as a `SimpleGraph`. Its literal negation does have a finite certificate: a
balanced `r`-colouring of `K_(r^2+1)`.

The lane nevertheless stopped before a discovery trial:

1. public fixed-case artifacts already claim `r = 5` through `r = 9`;
2. a separate public repository, frozen at
   `b0810a0ac878b0fd52c954125db4e98be919081d`, claims clean-room-audited
   computer-assisted proofs for `r = 10,11`;
3. four sub-60-second components of the `r = 10` package were independently
   replayed and regenerated byte-identical artifacts, while its roughly
   375-second terminal classification was not run under this campaign's cap;
4. those claims remain neither Lean-formalized nor externally peer reviewed,
   but they remove novelty and make counterexample hunting inappropriate;
5. the proposed affine `K_100` carrier does not exist, and a naive
   `Z/10` pseudo-affine replacement fails the balance premise;
6. exact calibration on valid prime-power affine carriers shows that the
   one-new-vertex extension is structurally impossible: required full lines in
   different directions intersect and demand different star colors at the
   same vertex.

Primary outcome: `KNOWN_PROOF_DOMAIN` for `r = 10,11` novelty and
`THEOREM_SHADOW` for the affine one-vertex transformation. No candidate
counterexample, release, issue, or PR is authorized.

## Method changes forced by the audit

1. Certificate shape is now a hard pre-ranking gate; additive wall scores
   cannot compensate for an asymptotic, existential, infinite, or fixed-answer
   mismatch.
2. Current-manifest scans operate on declarations, not `SimpleGraph` files.
3. Erdős status is stored as four coordinates with exact statement scope.
4. Novelty searches include newly created standalone proof repositories and
   releases, not only upstream issues and PRs.
5. Every scheduled carrier must have a frozen content hash and pass its own
   premise checks before solver work begins.
6. Calibration, development, and future held-out evidence remain separate.

The next discovery lane must be a genuinely unclaimed finite declaration with
a literal finite negation certificate, an exact residual bridge, and a valid
content-hashed carrier. Erdős 92 may proceed independently only as a known-
result formalization benchmark.
