# ErdosProblems/1212 — composite-anchor ray problem (structural audit)

**File:** `FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/1212.lean` @ `2411d22e`.
**Statement:** exists an injective unit-step path `f : ℕ → ℕ×ℕ` tending to infinity, all of whose vertices
are `Valid`: coordinates > 1, coprime, at least one coordinate composite. Open (weaker version solved by
C. Stewart via prime anchors; compositeness condition forbids them).

**Why it attracted attention:** the valid-vertex density near the diagonal `x = y+1` is high — every point
`(t+1, t)` with `t ≥ 2` is valid (consecutive integers are coprime, and one of two consecutive integers > 2
is composite). A "jog" construction alternates between diagonals `D_c = {(t+c, t)}`:
from `D_1(t)` one exits right when `t` is odd, down-left when `t` is even, and from `D_2(t−1)` an escape
to `D_3` always exists (of the two candidate steps, at most one has gcd divisible by 3). This suggests a
finite residue-class automaton could build an explicit certified ray — an affirmative resolution.
